Payment Review Checklist

1. Confirm the payment referenceMatch the reference against the transaction initiated by the customer.
2. Verify with PaystackUse server-side verification, not a customer screenshot or browser-submitted status.
3. Check the amount and currencyConfirm the paid amount matches the expected amount and NGN currency.
4. Prevent duplicate creditUse an idempotent server-side process so one payment cannot credit a wallet twice.
