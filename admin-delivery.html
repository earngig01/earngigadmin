Delivery Rules

Reference for the operating rules agreed for the EarnGig delivery network.

Partner verification

All riders and storekeepers must submit an application and complete verification.
Verification may include phone calls, interviews and requests for additional documents.
No partner may access delivery operations before admin approval.

Vendor pickup and delivery

A rider must collect an accepted vendor order before the end of the same calendar day.
The maximum delivery period is five calendar days from the original order release date.
A handover does not restart the five-day delivery deadline.
Delivery completion requires customer confirmation-code verification.

Collection rider and storekeeper

Collection riders group parcels by destination state and town.
Each bulk shipment is assigned to a registered, approved storekeeper.
The storekeeper confirms actual physical receipt and parcel count.
Confirmed bulk shipments split into individual customer parcels.
Collection rider earnings are payable after verified storekeeper receipt.
Storekeeper earnings are payable per successfully verified customer pickup.

Wallet and withdrawal rules

Normal rider, collection rider and storekeeper wallets are separate Naira wallets.
All three delivery wallet withdrawals have a 2% service charge.
Storekeepers have no minimum withdrawal amount.
Earnings must only be credited after the relevant handover or customer collection is securely verified.
